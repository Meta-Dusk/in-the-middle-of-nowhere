# In the Middle of Nowhere

This is a simple horror game made with Godot, originally for a friend of mine.

## Synopsis

You are an unemployed person whose desperate to find a job.
Eventually, you found a job offering for a convenience store clerk stationed
beside a gas station. The work times didn't deter you,
as night shifts aren't a deal breaker. However,
you did question why the location is basically in the middle of nowhere.
